SCIENTIFIC EVIDENCE DATABASE

EVIDENCE 1
Recommendation: Introduce cover crops or legume intercropping
Source organization: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO)
Source: Soil organic cover - Conservation Agriculture
Evidence: FAO explains that cover crops can improve soil properties, protect soil during fallow periods, recycle nutrients, improve soil structure, and increase biodiversity in agro-ecosystems.
Relevant metrics: Soil organic carbon, soil moisture, soil structure, biodiversity
Source URL: https://www.fao.org/conservation-agriculture/in-practice/soil-organic-cover/en/


EVIDENCE 2
Recommendation: Improve water conservation
Source organization: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO)
Source: The importance of soil organic matter
Evidence: FAO explains that soil organic matter affects soil structure, moisture-holding capacity, infiltration, and biological activity. Crop residues can improve infiltration, reduce evaporation, and increase soil moisture.
Relevant metrics: Soil moisture, water availability, organic matter
Source URL: https://www.fao.org/4/a0100e/a0100e08.htm


EVIDENCE 3
Recommendation: Increase crop and habitat diversity
Source organization: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO)
Source: The 10 Elements of Agroecology - Diversity
Evidence: FAO describes diversification through intercropping, crop rotations, agroforestry and other approaches as a way to increase biological diversity and support ecosystem functions such as pollination and nutrient cycling.
Relevant metrics: Species richness, habitat diversity, pollinator resources
Source URL: https://www.fao.org/agroecology/overview/the-10-elements-of-agroecology/diversity/en/


EVIDENCE 4
Recommendation: Increase crop and habitat diversity
Source organization: Scientific Advances / peer-reviewed research
Source: Agricultural diversification promotes multiple ecosystem services without compromising yield
Evidence: A review of 98 meta-analyses and 5,160 original studies found that agricultural diversification enhanced biodiversity and ecosystem services including pollination, pest control, nutrient cycling, soil fertility and water regulation, although responses were context-dependent.
Relevant metrics: Biodiversity, pollination, soil fertility, water regulation
Source URL: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.aba1715


EVIDENCE 5
Recommendation: Cover crops
Source organization: Agriculture, Ecosystems & Environment
Source: Soil organic carbon fractions in response to soil, environmental and agronomic factors under cover cropping systems: A global meta-analysis
Evidence: A global meta-analysis reported increases in several soil organic carbon fractions under cover cropping, with the magnitude varying according to soil, environmental and management conditions.
Relevant metrics: Soil organic carbon, microbial biomass carbon
Source URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.agee.2023.108591


EVIDENCE 6
Recommendation: Create habitat corridors and native vegetation patches
Source organization: Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO)
Source: Crop diversification for pollinator conservation
Evidence: FAO summarizes research indicating that intercropping, flowering crops and other diversification measures can increase floral resources and landscape connectivity that benefit pollinators, while noting that effects can vary among species.
Relevant metrics: Pollinator biodiversity, habitat connectivity, habitat diversity
Source URL: https://www.fao.org/agroecology/in-action/detail/crop-diversification-for-pollinator-conservation/en/
