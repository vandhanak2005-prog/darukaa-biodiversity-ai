LAND USE KNOWLEDGE

Land use and land cover influence habitat availability, habitat connectivity and biodiversity.

Agricultural intensification and monoculture can reduce habitat complexity and ecological connectivity.

Increasing vegetation diversity through crop diversification, intercropping, agroforestry, native vegetation patches and habitat corridors can provide greater habitat resources and improve ecological connectivity.

Land-use recommendations should consider soil health, rainfall, temperature, water availability and biodiversity together.

Relevant metrics:
Land use, habitat diversity, habitat connectivity, species richness, biodiversity
