CLIMATE AND WATER KNOWLEDGE

Rainfall and temperature influence water availability, plant growth and ecosystem processes.

Low rainfall can increase water stress, particularly when soil moisture is also low.

Soil organic matter can contribute to improved soil structure and water-holding capacity.

Water availability affects plant survival and can influence the availability of resources for animals and other organisms.

Climate conditions should be considered together with soil and land-use conditions rather than independently.

Important relationships:

Low rainfall + low soil moisture
→ increased water stress.

Low rainfall + low soil organic carbon
→ potentially lower soil resilience to dry conditions.

Vegetation diversity and appropriate land management can help improve ecosystem resilience, although the effectiveness depends on local climate and soil conditions.
