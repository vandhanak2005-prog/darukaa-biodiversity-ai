SOIL HEALTH KNOWLEDGE

Soil organic carbon is an important indicator of soil health. Higher soil organic carbon can improve soil structure, water retention, nutrient cycling and biological activity.

Low soil organic carbon can reduce soil quality and resilience, particularly when combined with low soil moisture and intensive cultivation.

Cover crops can increase organic inputs to soil and can support soil organisms. Legume-based cover crops can also contribute nitrogen to agricultural systems through biological nitrogen fixation.

Soil pH influences nutrient availability and microbial processes. Many agricultural crops perform well in moderately acidic to neutral soils, although the suitable pH range depends on the crop and soil.

Soil moisture influences plant growth and soil biological activity. Maintaining adequate soil moisture can improve plant survival and support soil organisms.

Important relationships:

Low organic carbon + low soil moisture
→ lower soil biological activity and reduced soil resilience.

Organic matter + adequate moisture
→ improved soil structure and biological activity.
